Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your ARNReady account and the personal data associated with it at any time. Here's exactly what happens and how to ask for it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to asmtechdeveloper@gmail.com from the email address registered on your ARNReady account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete my ARNReady account".
  3. We'll confirm your identity against the registered email and process the deletion request.
  4. You'll receive a confirmation email once your account and associated data have been deleted.

Requests are typically processed within a few business days.

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Email address, name, sign-in credentialsYes
Chapter progress, quiz scores, mock test historyYes
Bookmarks and saved dataYes
Premium unlock (isPaid) statusYes

What we may retain

Where legally required — for example, transaction and payment records needed for tax and accounting purposes — ASM Tech may retain a limited record of your purchase for as long as required by applicable law, even after your account is deleted. This retained data is limited to what's needed for compliance and is not used for any other purpose.

Note: deleting your account is permanent. Your quiz history, chapter progress, and Prepometer scores cannot be recovered once deletion is complete. If you've purchased premium access, deleting your account does not entitle you to a refund.

Questions?

Reach out to asmtechdeveloper@gmail.com for anything related to your data or this process.
