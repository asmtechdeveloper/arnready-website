Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

ARNReady is built and operated by Anusha Murthy, trading as ASM Tech, Bengaluru, India. If you have questions about this policy, email asmtechdeveloper@gmail.com.

What we collect

If you sign in:

  • Your name and email address from Google or your registered email
  • Your display name if you choose to set one
  • Your exam date if you choose to enter it

Automatically:

  • Your progress through chapters and mock tests
  • Questions you answered correctly and incorrectly
  • App usage via Google AdMob (for free users — ad performance data)
  • Crash and performance data via Firebase

We never collect:

  • Your payment card details — payments are handled entirely by Google Play or Razorpay
  • Your location
  • Your contacts or any data outside ARNReady

How we use it

  • To save your progress across sessions and devices
  • To show you your Prepometer score and weak areas
  • To serve relevant ads to free users via Google AdMob
  • To fix bugs and improve the app

With your permission: when you register, we will ask if you'd like to receive occasional updates from ARNReady — new features, exam tips, and study resources. We will only send these if you explicitly opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email we send. We will never sell or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third parties

  • Google Firebase — authentication and data storage
  • Google AdMob — advertising for free users
  • Razorpay — payment processing (website only)

None of these parties receive your data for their own marketing purposes beyond their standard platform terms.

Your data, your rights

  • You can delete your account and all associated data by emailing asmtechdeveloper@gmail.com
  • You can request a copy of your data at any time
  • Paid users are ad-free — AdMob does not run on paid accounts

Children

ARNReady is intended for adults preparing for a professional certification. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify you within the app. Continued use after notification means you accept the updated policy.
